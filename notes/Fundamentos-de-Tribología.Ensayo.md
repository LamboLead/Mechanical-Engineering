## Interactions on atomic scale and superlubricity

- The review _'Influence Factors on Mechanisms of Superlubricity in DLC Films'_  is regarted to be one of the most fundamental achievements over the last 25 years. Which shows that a pure mechanical analysis of a tribological system is not sufficient to explain superlubricity of DLC films.
- In a paper, Zhou discusses if it is possible to use macroscopic contact mechanics at the level when thermal fluctuations start playing an important role. The fluctuations are almost equivalent to a constant shift of the surface, which is 1/1.5 of the sqrt fluctuation of the free surface.
- In the paper _'Contacts with negative work of adhesion and superlubricity'_, the author concludes that there are two main properties that determine friction between two materials: Adhesion and 'compatibility' of materials. The less compatible (the tendency to form allows), and the less the adhesion, the smaller the friction. Furthermore, the adhesion should be negative to have the effect of 'superlubricity'. Also, two materials are incompatible if their lattices are incommensurate (and thus, non-intermixable).
- Along with another paper of RT by Fang, which illustrates the mechanism in the special case of hydration-lubricated contacts, which provide the uniquely low coefficient of friction in natural joints.
