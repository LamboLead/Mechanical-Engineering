The main purpose of an accumulator is to store energy, in this case, by compressing a gas, in the form of potential energy

**Applications:**

- Provide pressurized fluid during brief high-demand period.
- Absorb pressure surges and releasing them in smooth pulsations.

![](/assets/images/2025-05-10-16-25-01.png)

> When designing circuits with accumulators, you should put the accumulator as near as possible to the inlet of the valve, so that the input pressure is as smooth as possible.
