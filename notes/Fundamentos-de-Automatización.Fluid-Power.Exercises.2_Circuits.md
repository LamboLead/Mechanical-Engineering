1. How does temperature influence the force and volumetric efficiencies of a cylinder?

  **Ans:** The viscosity of a fluid is inversely proportional to its temperature. This means that when a fluid is hotter, it decreases its viscosity, and:
   - The mechanical efficiency of the cylinder is increased, because there are less viscous forces acting against the movement of the cylinder rod.
   - The volumetric efficiency of the cylinder is decreased, because the fluid is more prone to leakage events.
